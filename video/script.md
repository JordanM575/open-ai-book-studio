## Notes for the read

- First person for the agent, second person for the author. No “we” except for storage and privacy.
- Pause after “the book stays yours” and before the close.
- Say “Markdown” plainly, not as jargon. Say “checked save” once; do not explain the hash.
- Do not claim a feature the recorded build does not show. If a shot is cut, cut its line.
- The `$0.01` estimate is a Muse Image price from the event documentation; confirm it in the build
  before recording.

## Disclosure

If the narration is synthetic, add to the video description:

> Narration generated with [provider and voice]. Captions transcribed with Muse Voice Transcribe.
> Music: [track and licence].
