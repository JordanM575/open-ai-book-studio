# Product acceptance gates

This is a traceability register, not a record of passed evidence or accepted decisions. The canonical
data is [product-acceptance-gates.json](product-acceptance-gates.json), linked from the lane graph in
[product-completion.json](product-completion.json). The table below is generated from that data and
names every one of the 63 solid and three independent conditional relationships. Owner links lead to
the relevant contracts, not to an assertion that their tasks have been completed.

A solid edge means the **applicable upstream contract** is a prerequisite to acceptance of the
downstream lane's whole retained scope. It is not an order requiring every upstream feature to finish
before an independent downstream slice begins. A conditional edge applies only to its stated claim;
the three conditions do not enable or depend on each other. The refusal column says what must not be
claimed when that contract or evidence is absent. Satisfying an edge does not, by itself, accept the
whole lane, authorize an open decision, prove runtime quality, or approve a release.

L23 is scoped to its explicitly supported desktop capability and target matrix. An omitted or
unclaimed capability may be absent from that desktop slice, but cannot thereby close its **full
retained lane** or L24. L24 separately requires direct retained-scope acceptance of **each of
L00–L23**, subject only to an explicit author-approved scope change. The eight arrows entering L24
are sequencing relationships; neither those arrows nor transitive reachability are the direct
conjunction. They do not add 24 arrows to the graph or create a new permission to defer scope. See
the [map design rationale](PRODUCT-MAP-DESIGN.md) and [retained follow-through](RETAINED-DELIVERY.md).

## Maintenance and validation

Edit the JSON records and graph together when the actual contract changes, preserving the independent
conditional boundaries and resolving any affected product decisions separately. Regenerate this table
with `python3 scripts/validate-completion-specs.py --write-gate-table`, then run
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 scripts/validate-completion-specs.py`. The validator checks the
edge/condition bijection, nonempty semantics, linked lane owners, table agreement, direct terminal
membership and the existing graph, ownership, execution and Mermaid invariants. It does not judge
whether prose is substantively sufficient, evidence exists or passed, an implementation works, a
decision was accepted, or an author approved release.
