# Workspace and dialog audit

**Date:** 2026-10-25
**Build:** web renderer at `apps/web/dist` over localhost; the same renderer ships in Electron
**Method:** `agent-browser` snapshots and DOM measurements (`visual-check`), plus the
`ui-ux-pro-max` UX rules. Every dialog was opened at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.
No screenshots were read into the conversation; the checks are numeric.

## What was wrong

1. **Settings were unreachable from the workspace.** Provider connections lived behind a gear
   in the Library only. The workspace header had search, Story Bible, export, theme, the
   agent, and the model picker, and the phone More sheet had no settings entry. The Library
   gear was also labelled “Connections”, so the name did not match the icon.
2. **Save state was colour-only on phones.** Below 1000px the label was `sr-only`, leaving a
   green, amber, or red dot as the only signal.
3. **Chapter delete was hover-only.** The action had `opacity-0` and appeared on
   `group-hover`; touch devices have no hover, so it was invisible in the phone drawer.
4. **Long dialog actions scrolled away.** Settings and Story Bible put Done/Save inside the
   scrolling body; on a 393 × 852 screen the action sat below the fold.

## What changed

- `SettingsDialog` replaces `ConnectionsDialog` and covers **Appearance** (System / Light /
  Dark with `aria-pressed`), **Provider connections**, and **Library** (where the files live).
  It opens from the Library gear, the workspace header, and the phone More sheet. The gear is
  now labelled Settings in both places.
- The workspace save state always shows a short text label (`Saved`, `Unsaved`, `Saving…`,
  `Not saved`); the full label with the time remains in `aria-label`.
- The chapter delete action is visible at 0.65 opacity on touch and in the mobile drawer, at
  the 44 × 44 touch target.
- `DialogContent` accepts a footer outside the scroll area. Settings and Story Bible keep
  their actions visible while the body scrolls.
- Settings is 640px wide from tablet up; the phone layout stays full width minus 24px gutters.

## Not covered

- VoiceOver order and labels, keyboard-only traversal beyond Radix's focus trap, reduced
  motion/transparency.
- Landscape iPad, real devices, and long book titles in the 700–999px header.
- Provider connection and key-entry flows, which need the desktop app and a real account.
- A full contrast pass in both themes; the audit checked layout, focus containment, and target
  size, not every token pair.
